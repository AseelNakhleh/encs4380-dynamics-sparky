THE LEARNING LOOP

Observe. Estimate. Verify.

01

Read the shape

A slowly rising curve suggests a large time constant. Overshoot indicates an underdamped second-order system.

02

Find useful landmarks

Use the 63.2% point for first-order steps, phase lag for sines, or peak overshoot and time for second-order steps.

03

Test your model

After submitting, compare the theoretical response of your estimate to the true curve. Iterate on a new sensor.

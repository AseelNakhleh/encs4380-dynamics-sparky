HOW THE SIMULATION WORKS

Inside the feedback loop

The error is e = r − y. A PID controller calculates its output from proportional, accumulated-error, and derivative terms. The page steps the plant in time with Euler integration (Δt = 0.004 s), measures the response, and tests the stated limits.

u = Kpe + Ki∫e dt − Kdd(y)/dt

Derivative-on-measurement uses a small filter to avoid setpoint derivative kick; conditional integration prevents actuator windup. “Zero error” is implemented as ≤1% maximum tracking error over the final 2 seconds because finite-time numerical simulations cannot prove exact zero asymptotically.
